# Battery (ordered Oct 6, 2026, arriving Oct 8)

JLJLUP LP103665, Amazon 4-pack ($25.99): one per board (Joe's, the gift unit,
a spare board) plus a spare battery.

- 3.7 V nominal, 4.2 V full, 3.0 V empty. 3000 mAh claimed (11.1 Wh); expect
  nearer 2500 mAh in practice, about 7 to 9 hours with the screen bright.
- Size: 36 W x 10 H x 67 L mm per the seller's drawing (the listing text says
  65 L; the case is sized for 67). 49 g. Lead 60 to 70 mm.
- Built-in protection circuit (overcharge, over-discharge, short circuit).
- Max charge and discharge 1C (3 A). Charge only between 0 and 45 C.
- Plug: JST 1.25 mm pitch, 2-pin, housing 4.3 mm wide. Red = +, black = -.
  The seller warns the plug orientation is NOT universal.

## Board side
The board's BAT plug sits on the back, on the bottom edge near the BOOT
button. Seen from the back with that edge at the bottom, the silkscreen marks
are "-" on the left and "+" on the right. The board's other plugs are also
1.25 mm (its bundled cable is "1.25mm to DuPont"), so the battery plug should
fit, but confirm with a ruler photo.

## Before the first plug-in (do it with Joe)
1. Photo of the BAT plug next to a ruler, marks visible.
2. Hold the battery plug next to the board socket the way it would go in.
   The red wire must line up with the board's "+" mark.
3. If it's reversed, do not force or plug it in. The two crimp pins can be
   swapped in the housing (lift the small tab, slide the pin out) - walk Joe
   through it step by step.
